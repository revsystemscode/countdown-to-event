¿Cuántos días faltan para tu viaje, el cumpleaños de tu mejor amiga o ese concierto que llevas meses esperando? Countdown to Event te lo dice de un vistazo.

CUENTAS REGRESIVAS BONITAS
• Crea un evento en segundos: título, emoji, fecha y hora.

## English (en-US)

**App name** — 18
```
Countdown to Event
```

**Short description** — 76
```
Count down the days to birthdays, trips and more, with widgets and reminders
```

**Full description**
```
How many days until your trip, your best friend's birthday or that concert you've waited months for? Countdown to Event tells you at a glance.

BEAUTIFUL COUNTDOWNS
• Create an event in seconds: title, emoji, date and time.
• Pick a color, a gradient or your own photo as the background.
• Multi-day events like trips or festivals.
• Repeat daily, weekly, every 2 weeks, monthly or yearly — perfect for birthdays and anniversaries.

HOME SCREEN WIDGETS
• Countdown widget in small or wide size.
• Live mode with hours, minutes and seconds.
• List widget with your upcoming events.

REMINDERS
• Get notified 15 minutes, 1 hour, 1 or 2 days before.
• And on the day itself: "Today is the day!"

SHARE THE EXCITEMENT
• Share an image of your countdown on WhatsApp, Instagram or anywhere.

YOUR DATA IS YOURS
• No ads, no accounts.
• Everything stays on your phone; the app doesn't connect to the internet.
• Export and import your events, photos included, to move to a new phone.

Plus: dark mode, large text support, English and Spanish.
```
